MISSION

Three promises that connect scattered researchers

Aerospace labs run on large, project-based teams — even people in the same subfield rarely cross paths. Founded in 2024 by four graduate students at the University of Tokyo, the chapter stands on three goals.

01

Build the network

A human network of Korean aerospace researchers in Japan, across universities and institutes. The starting point of everything we do.

02

Research & fellowship

Research exchange that helps each of us grow, and friendships that keep the community sustainable — through seminars, talks, and meetups.

03

Cross-field fusion

Joint events with other chapters and the annual symposium widen our circle and contribute to science as a whole.

RESEARCH SPECTRUM

We define "aerospace" broadly

A single satellite carries propulsion, thermal, structures, power, communications, and control at once. If your field can extend to aerospace — atmosphere, environment, fluids, data — it belongs here.
